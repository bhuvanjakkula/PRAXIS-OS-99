# Payment record integrity review

Integrated 4 October 2026. Most supplied themes are already addressed by retrospective adjustments, invoice/receipt evidence, audit integrity and supplier review. New notes cover independent payment-detail change confirmation and settlement-channel reconciliation. Either note enables both checks plus linked supplier, ledger and audit evidence.

Payment-detail verification should reference confirmation through a previously trusted independent contact and role-separated approval, rather than treating the change-request email as proof. Settlement evidence should identify authorized and emergency channels, transaction references, currency/date bases and bank-ledger reconciliation. Store restricted-system references rather than unnecessary account credentials or personal information.

Cryptographic integrity can help detect changes to recorded data under a defined trust model; it cannot prove physical delivery, truthful original timestamps, authorized bank ownership or absence of off-system transactions. Timely posting does not eliminate fraud, and delayed posting is not itself evidence of fraud. The supplied corporate-loss figures and survey results remain unverified/context-specific research leads and are not embedded in this product.

The implementation records supplied review evidence and flags gaps. It performs no identity lookup, bank-detail validation, currency reconciliation or settlement. Payment destination verification remains false. Original summary prose and figures are not reproduced.
