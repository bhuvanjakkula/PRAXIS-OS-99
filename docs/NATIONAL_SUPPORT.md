# Country leadership and national planning

Country leadership adds separate public-policy, economic-policy,
international-relations and agreement-assessment areas. The broad catalog has
56 presets, including India, United States, China, United Kingdom, Germany,
France, Japan, Brazil, Canada, Australia, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa,
Russia and others. Custom entries are accepted. This is not an objective ranking
of countries or a complete world registry.

Every plan records country, assessment date, period, sources, objective,
authorization references, affected groups, partners and assumptions. Country
selection labels the context; no current political, constitutional, economic
or treaty facts are inferred from it. Users must supply dated country-specific
evidence and obtain responsible review.

Optional economic calculations use one-period nominal GDP growth, opening debt,
effective interest rate, projected revenues and primary spending. An adverse
scenario applies declared growth, revenue reduction and spending increase.
Outputs include GDP, primary/overall balance, interest cost, borrowing need,
projected debt and debt/GDP. Deficits add to debt; surpluses reduce it with a zero
floor. These transparent identities are not a macroeconomic forecast or a debt
sustainability assessment and exclude maturities, foreign-exchange movements,
contingent liabilities and behavioral feedback. Economic inputs are never
prefilled with invented country values.

Agreement records retain exact titles, partners, clauses, references, review
dates and self-reported status/action consistency. Review dates cannot postdate
the assessment. A pass for an agreement reported in force remains unverified;
unknown/proposed/conflicting assessments require review. No agreement is signed,
interpreted authoritatively, amended or transmitted.

Saved plans show conditional warnings, evidence gaps, policy/diplomatic questions
and next steps, support country filtering and JSON export, and load policy
criteria into Decision compute. Numerical option scores, constraints and evidence
must still be supplied. This is neutral public-policy preparation, with no voter
targeting, campaigning or autonomous state action.

Tests verify fiscal identities, optional inputs, growth validation, agreement
dates/statuses, tenant isolation, roles, revision conflicts and persistence.
Browser checks cover country/custom selections, agreement entry, economic
scenarios, country filtering, reloads, decision-engine handoff and mobile layout.
