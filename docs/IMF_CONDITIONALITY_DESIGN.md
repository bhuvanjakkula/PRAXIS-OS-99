# Social-protection conditionality design review

Integrated 4 October 2026 from the supplied research leads; the original Consensus narrative is not reproduced. Reform recommendations are proposals, not descriptions of universal current IMF practice.

## Evidence and qualification

The [IMF conditionality factsheet](https://www.imf.org/en/about/factsheets/sheets/2023/imf-conditionality) distinguishes prior actions, quantitative performance criteria and indicative targets. It explains that the Executive Board may waive a missed performance criterion when satisfied the program will succeed. Upgrading a target does not automatically ensure implementation or beneficiary protection.

The [2024 operational social-spending guidance](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/007/2024/022/article-A001-en.xml) already allows different quantitative and structural tools where social spending is critical to program objectives. Thus the supplied suggestion to introduce stronger targets should be evaluated against actual program definitions and authority rather than assuming that all existing floors are legally identical.

The [2025 expenditure-conditionality working paper](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/wp/issues/2025/01/23/designing-expenditure-policy-conditionality-in-imf-supported-programs-561194) supports tailored design and attention to implementation constraints. It is research, not a binding policy decision. The [2024 social-protection study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12992-024-01045-9) identifies spending-floor limitations in its historical sample. Its findings do not establish effects of every current program. Other citations in the pasted summary remain leads unless independently reviewed.

## Added workflow

Optional `conditionality` evidence in IMF pilot proposals records target classification plus six review areas: ex-ante social/gender assessment; country ownership and alternatives; frontline workforce and essential investment; coverage/access and appeals; monitoring partnership and resources; and exact authority, target definition, waiver/cure and service continuity. An empty area remains a visible gap. A target label or completed notes do not establish binding status or authorize a lending action. Agency roles must be agreed rather than implied by entering ILO or UNICEF names.

The spending comparison additionally displays `baseline_spending * current_price_index / baseline_price_index`, the nominal amount equivalent to baseline purchasing power. This is an analytical comparator, not an adopted spending floor or legal entitlement. Existing supplied nominal-floor attainment and actual constant-price change remain separate. Comparable periods, scopes, units and index bases must be assessed by a reviewer.

## Proposed solutions requiring further evaluation

Evaluate inflation-indexed protection, workforce-cap alternatives, universal versus targeted coverage, essential-investment protection and public financial management together with financing feasibility, administrative capacity and distributional evidence. Adopt no automatic tranche suspension: assess authority, waiver procedures, borrower participation and potential service harm. Technology supports evidence coordination; institutional parties retain policy and financing decisions.
