# Delayed-posting procurement review

Integrated 4 October 2026 from supplied research leads. Adds retrospective-adjustment evidence, three-way matching evidence and payable-queue review to the IMF workflow. Any posting note enables all three checks plus linked audit integrity, procurement exception and resolution evidence.

Retrospective review distinguishes economic-event, document and posting dates and retains original entries, reasons and approval evidence. Matching review links purchase orders, independently verified receipts and invoices, including quantities, amounts, substitutions and unresolved exceptions. Queue review records due dates, disputes, cash constraints, changes in priority and supplier/service effects.

Late entries may reflect outages, corrections or legitimate accounting adjustments. They are risk indicators, not findings of fraud, unlawful backdating or vendor misconduct. A receipt reference is not proof of delivery; ageing alone does not establish arrears without contractual due dates and dispute context. Avoid automatically deleting or penalizing retrospective entries: preserve provenance and refer disputed evidence to authorized reviewers.

The supplied statistical amounts and percentages remain unverified research leads and are not product assumptions. Existing primary context includes [World Bank FMIS transaction analytics](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/226121546531748578/pdf/WPS8689.pdf) and the [2020 Zambia procurement study](https://www.scirp.org/pdf/ojbm_2020033016291924.pdf), whose findings are context-specific rather than universal causal effects.

This implementation saves evidence and flags gaps; it does not perform invoice matching, ingest payable records, alter payment queues or determine fraud. The supplied narrative and figures are not reproduced.
