The Dewey Inquiry Engine
Instead of asking an LLM:
“Give me the answer.”

PRAXIS asks:
1. What exactly is problematic?
The original question may itself be badly framed.
2. What do we actually observe?
Separate evidence from assumptions and interpretations.
3. What relationships could explain the situation?
Construct causal hypotheses.
4. What possible responses exist?
Generate alternatives rather than prematurely selecting one.
5. What consequences would each produce?
Simulation and scenario analysis.
6. What can we test safely?
Turn reasoning into experiments.
7. What happened?
Capture actual outcomes.
8. What should change in our model?
Reconstruct beliefs, assumptions and relationships.
