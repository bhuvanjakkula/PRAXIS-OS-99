# IFMIS reporting and ceiling review

Integrated 4 October 2026. The supplied research is treated as leads; survey correlations and jurisdiction-specific effect sizes are not imported as universal causal assumptions. The original narrative/figures are not redistributed.

The evidence workflow adds system coverage, reporting latency, approval/override controls and cash-rationing equity. Any IFMIS note enables all four reviews plus links to commitment controls, ledger reconciliation and exception resolution. Reported real-time status remains unverified; the app has no live IFMIS feed or automated payment/rejection authority.

Coverage evidence should identify connected agencies/modules, denominator, manual procurement and retrospective entry. Latency evidence should distinguish transaction event, entry, posting and reconciliation time, including outages. Approval evidence should document funds checks, role separation and preserved overrides. Equity evidence should distinguish appropriation from cash allotment and identify delays, arrears and service impacts across sectors and regions.

Expenditure ceilings are upper limits, not protected spending minima. Staying below a ceiling cannot demonstrate that a floor was funded or that a facility received funds. Faster reporting does not establish accurate reconciliation, full coverage or administrative enforcement. Reviewers should test bypasses and delays before relying on alerts or considering institutional remedies.

Primary support: the [World Bank FMIS overview](https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/governance/brief/financial-management-information-systems-fmis) describes integration of budget execution, accounting and reporting. [IEG's IFMIS evaluation](https://ieg.worldbankgroup.org/evaluations/public-financial-and-debt-management/chapter-5-supporting-integrated-financial) shows that sophisticated technology can coexist with limited transaction coverage and weak ex-ante control. These motivate coverage validation rather than assuming that system adoption proves enforcement.
