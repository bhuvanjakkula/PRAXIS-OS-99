# Aviation and maritime incident support — 2026-10-01

Separate Aircrew support and Ship captain support pages accept any declared
platform type. They provide structured observations, crew and passenger status,
position/time, environmental context, communication status, exact procedure
references, missing-information flags and downloadable incident handovers.
Records are stored within the active decision, bound to its revision and filtered
by aviation/maritime domain. Role, tenant and stale-revision checks apply.

These are training, preparation and incident-documentation tools. They do not
provide certified operational guidance, emergency maneuvers, navigation, system
diagnosis, vessel/aircraft control or safety guarantees. No telemetry is connected.
Complete reports remain explicitly unverified. Applicability confirmation is a
crew declaration and requires a procedure reference. Actual emergencies retain
crew authority and must use approved procedures and relevant emergency services.

Authoritative framework references:
- FAA: https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap6_section_1.html
- IMO: https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/humanelement/pages/ismcode.aspx

Operational deployment across all types remains unimplemented: it requires exact
manufacturer/operator manuals, configuration and revision controls, validated
sensor interfaces, human-factors evaluation and applicable certification.
