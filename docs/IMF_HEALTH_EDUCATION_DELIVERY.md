# Health and education delivery evidence

Integrated 4 October 2026. The supplied summary is a research lead; its narrative and figures are not redistributed. Numerical effects cited there are not adopted as universal product assumptions or causal estimates.

Praxis OS adds optional conditionality evidence for four operational areas: allocation/release/expenditure reconciliation (including dates, denominators and arrears); independently verified facility receipts and follow-up; health/education/finance co-financing responsibilities; and staffing, supplies, infrastructure, household costs and service-quality outcomes. Any delivery note enables all four checks plus links to workforce, access and monitoring evidence. Gaps remain visible in saved and exported proposals. This is an evidence review workflow, not an automated treasury or facility-data integration.

The [World Bank budget-alignment research](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/e750b527-c975-54f0-b666-98e623a8a747/download) motivates connecting health financing with government budget processes. The [2023 school-based collaboration paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/public-health/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2023.1241594/full) proposes inter-ministerial joint financing or time-limited co-financing and emphasizes ministry commitments. These do not establish that every school-based program will improve outcomes.

The pasted claim that binding criteria guarantee governments cannot bypass targets is too strong: [IMF guidance](https://www.imf.org/en/about/factsheets/sheets/2023/imf-conditionality) allows Board-approved waivers and overall program review. Missing targets must not automatically trigger service-affecting action in this software. Allocation, release, expenditure and verified service delivery are distinct evidence stages; adequate spending alone does not establish learning, health outcomes or beneficiary access. Reviewers retain responsibility for evidence quality, comparability and institutional decisions.
