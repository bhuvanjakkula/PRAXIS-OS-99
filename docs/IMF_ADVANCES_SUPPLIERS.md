# Advances, aggregated procurement and supplier review

Integrated 4 October 2026 from supplied research leads. Adds advance-recovery, procurement-aggregation and supplier-verification evidence. Any note enables all three checks and links to receipt/invoice matching, audit integrity and exception resolution.

Advance review records authority, guarantees, milestones, deductions, outstanding balances and due dates. Aggregation review records related needs/orders, review period, applicable thresholds and legitimate lot rationale. Supplier review records dated lawful registry/ownership/conflict checks, bank-detail changes, receipt evidence and disputed identities. Avoid recording unnecessary personal or bank-account details in free-text evidence; use restricted-system references where appropriate.

Advances can legitimately finance mobilization. Multiple lots may support competition, specialist delivery or small suppliers; fragmentation alone does not establish evasion. Registry mismatches can reflect data errors or changes, not fictitious entities. Delay and outstanding balances are review indicators, not findings of wrongdoing.

The supplied Ghanaian procurement paper, DOI 10.3846/jcem.2020.11659, is relevant to procurement irregularities. Financing, replenishment and payment-delay models in the wider reference list do not automatically establish fraud pathways in IFMIS. Supplied percentages remain unverified/context-specific leads and are not embedded as universal effects. Original summary prose/figures are not reproduced.

This release saves evidence and flags gaps; it does not calculate recoveries, aggregate live purchases, query registries or certify suppliers. Supplier legitimacy and procurement fraud remain undetermined.
