# IFMIS latency and preventive-control review

Integrated 4 October 2026. Most supplied themes overlap with the existing coverage, timestamp, approval and reconciliation review. Added evidence areas are offline backlog recovery, module handoffs and preventive funds-check verification. Any note enables these three checks plus linked reporting-latency, system-coverage and approval evidence.

Fast reporting is not sufficient to establish preventive control. Review evidence must show that an authorized check used the relevant balance and budget version before commitment, with preserved rejection/override records. A local commitment check may operate before a central reporting extract; reporting delay and control timing are distinct. Backlog evidence should identify age, volume, outages, duplicates and recovery reconciliation. Module evidence should trace procurement, budget, cash and ledger identifiers and failed/retried transfers.

Primary context remains the [IMF commitment-control note](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/tnm/issues/2016/12/31/commitment-controls-23302) and [IEG IFMIS evaluation](https://ieg.worldbankgroup.org/evaluations/public-financial-and-debt-management/chapter-5-supporting-integrated-financial). Their implementation and coverage concerns motivate evidence review, not a claim that reporting speed alone enforces budgets. Supplied survey correlations are not universal causal estimates, and this release does not claim consensus or reproduce the original research narrative.

This is a saved evidence-review extension. No live timestamps are collected or independently verified; no module synchronization, balance reservation, payment rejection or treasury action is executed. Preventive-control verification remains explicitly false.
