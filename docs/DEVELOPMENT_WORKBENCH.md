## Use

Choose a country, then select a workbench problem area. Document local conditions and measurement definitions in the national plan. Enable Development investment portfolio and enter a common valuation horizon, monetary unit, available budget, ethical priority multiplier, benefit-share floor and stress assumptions. Add up to twelve candidate projects with total present-value cost, benefit ranges, low-income benefit shares, evidence, mutually exclusive groups and six review gates. Assess every stored decision constraint for every project. Save the national plan to retain inputs, exclusions and results; exports include the portfolio.

The planner examines every subset of eligible projects, including the no-new-project baseline. Stressed cost must fit the budget; mutual exclusions and the low-income benefit-share floor are enforced. Failed or unknown legal/institutional gates or decision constraints exclude projects before optimization. Ranking uses stressed low-end benefits with an explicit low-income preference multiplier, minus stressed costs. Preference-weighted scores are not money, GDP, poverty headcounts or calibrated welfare gains. Low-income share is a share of valued benefits, not beneficiaries or measured inequality. The planner returns the ten highest scores, reports total feasible subsets and best-score ties, and does not execute policy.

At most 4,096 subsets are examined. A synthetic twelve-project benchmark on this machine completed in 0.0333 seconds. This is exact classical enumeration over a small bounded model, not quantum computing or proof of a globally optimal national policy. Existing Decision compute also supports up to 50,000 uncertainty draws within its workload cap.

## Review limits

The additive portfolio model cannot discover causal effects, dependency chains, overlapping benefits, displacement, financing availability, exchange-rate dynamics, implementation timing or administrative capacity. Use mutually exclusive groups for alternatives that cannot coexist; review more complex dependencies and interactions before interpreting a selected portfolio. All gates are supplied assessments, not certifications. Adverse endpoints are conservative conditional tests, not forecasts. Poor countries are not assumed to share one solution; distribution, rights, institutions and local evidence remain central.
